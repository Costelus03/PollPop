About Quick Poll

What this is

Quick Poll is a simple daily poll - one question, every day, similar in spirit to word games like Wordle. Vote once, see what everyone else picked, and come back tomorrow for a new question. No accounts, no sign-up - just vote and see the results.

How the questions are made

Being upfront about this: each day's question is generated with Claude, an AI assistant, based on a prompt written to keep questions varied and genuinely interesting - personal preferences, light opinions, "would you rather" style dilemmas, and the occasional fun hypothetical. Questions are reviewed from time to time to keep the quality up.

Why I built this

I wanted a small, low-effort way to see what people actually think about everyday things - the kind of question you'd bring up with friends, but with an instant, real answer from everyone who voted. No accounts, no clutter, just a question and a result.

Past polls

Every previous day's poll stays available - use the "Past polls" button on the main page to browse or jump to any date and see how people voted.
