What this site does

This site shows a daily poll question. When you vote, your choice is saved as an anonymous number in our database - we do not ask for your name, email, or any account.

Cookies and local storage we use

  • Necessary: we save which poll(s) you already voted on in your browser's local storage, so you can't vote twice on the same day.
  • Advertising: if you accept cookies, Google AdSense may store cookies to show and measure ads. If you decline, ads (if shown) will not be personalized.
  • Analytics: if enabled and accepted, basic, aggregated visit statistics may be collected to understand how the site is used.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data we hold about you, and the right to withdraw cookie consent at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.
